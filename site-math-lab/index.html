What the calculator counts

  • Visits = monthly searches × expected capture rate
  • Ads = page views ÷ 1,000 × your RPM
  • Subscription = new paid users × monthly price
  • Net = estimated revenue − monthly running costs

Before trusting a number

Validate the search term, check the current results page, and use RPM or conversion data from a source you trust. The subscription view estimates the first month's new subscribers; it does not model renewals or churn.
